Steadier sync, and a few finishing touches.

- **Factwright's version** now shows at the bottom of the sidebar, so you can tell an update has landed.
- **Sync checks your account's key** when you sign in again, and as it syncs, so two computers can't quietly end up on different keys.
- **The terms of service and privacy policy** are linked from the first step and from signing in to sync.
- **New installs** open on a window in Factwright's colors, on Mac and on Windows.
