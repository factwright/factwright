Filling in job applications knows more about you, ready for the browser extension's next version.

- **Your resumes fill what Profile leaves blank:** your name, contact details and where you work come from the job's own resume, or your newest. About you can fill in its blanks from your resume in one click.
- **Work history and education, in Profile:** every role you held, with several at one company kept apart, and each school. Your Claude fills them in from your fact bank, and forms that ask for each job and school get them.
- **Your Claude drafts what nothing on file answers:** with the extension's next version, Draft them with your Claude has your Claude write a form's own questions in Factwright, and the extension fills them in for you to check.
- **Several titles at one company:** each role stays its own, in your fact bank, on your resumes and in the ATS X-ray.
- **Sync no longer hangs** on Syncing… when the sync server stalls.
