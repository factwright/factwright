Prepare to apply for Greenhouse jobs, and Factwright's side of the browser extension's next version.

- **Prepare to apply:** for a job saved from Greenhouse, Get the form's questions brings its application questions into Factwright before you open the form, and your Claude drafts them, so the extension fills them in at once.
- **With the extension's next version, right in the form on the job sites you allow:** a small Fill button, and menus in the fields with Factwright's answers, as a password manager does. One optional permission covers the systems most companies use, and you can add any careers site.
- **Forms inside a company's careers page:** Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby forms shown in a frame fill like any other, and location boxes and Yes/No buttons are picked for you.
