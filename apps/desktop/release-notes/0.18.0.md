Analyze job, several tasks at once in tabs, and a working bar that shows what your Claude is doing.

- **Analyze job:** in place of the fit check, one run fills in a job's posting if it's missing, puts its title, company, pay and place right, merges a job you saved twice, checks the fit, and suggests the pay to ask for.
- **On its own:** your Claude analyzes the jobs you save while it's free, with no notifications, and steps aside for anything you start. With sync, only one of your computers analyzes each job. Your Claude → Settings → Tasks at once turns it off.
- **Several tasks at once:** each task runs in a tab of its own, up to three at a time, and the rest wait in line and start by themselves.
- **Analyze for picked jobs:** select jobs, and one run analyzes them all, its tab saying how far it's got, such as "Analyze · 2 of 6 done".
- **Edit details:** change a job's company, role, pay and place. What you set wins over the posting, and your Claude never changes it.
- **The working strip:** a small picture of what your Claude is doing, where it is on its to-do list, such as "Step 3 of 7", and its helpers at work.
- **With the browser extension's next version:** Undo once it marks a job applied, and your resume and cover letter go up under their own names.
