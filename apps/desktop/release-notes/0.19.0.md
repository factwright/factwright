Find jobs reaches far more jobs in any field, you can tell your Claude what to change in Profile, and application forms fit the job you're applying to.

- **Find jobs in any field:** your Claude works out the job titles and places to try for your kind of work, then reads what employers have open today on the systems hospitals, schools, banks and shops use, such as Workday and iCIMS, as well as Greenhouse and Lever. It can search USAJOBS and Workable too, and a job it can't confirm is still open stays in New, saying so.
- **Fit scores suit more kinds of work:** Stack is now Skills, which counts licenses and methods as well as tools, and a job's industry counts only when the posting asks for it.
- **Look on LinkedIn and Indeed yourself:** Find jobs has ready-made searches for your job titles and places, which open in your own browser when you click them.
- **Update with Claude:** on What you want, Why you're leaving, Beyond work, your work history, your starting resumes, your templates and your fact bank, say what to change in your own words. Your Claude changes that part and nothing else, and its task lists each change, with Undo.
- **Forms that fit the job:** the box about what you did in a job fills with words for the job you're applying to, with the others in its menu. For a job not under a kind of job yet, that box and the pay to ask for go by the job's title, and say so. The browser extension's 0.3.1 does the same through sync.
- **Themes:** choose how Factwright looks with Appearance, in the sidebar, including Tokyo Night, Nord and Catppuccin.
- **A job's details can be any width:** drag the edge beside the job list.
- **What's new:** the first time Factwright opens after an update, it says what's new in a few plain lines.
- **Commas in titles and cities:** "Director, Sales" and "Chicago, IL" each stay one entry. Press Enter to add them.
- **Claude Code 2.1.295 or newer:** Factwright now needs it, and each run has only the built-in tools its task uses. With an older one, Factwright says what to run, including for Homebrew and WinGet.
