Updates that are easier to see, and one restart to get the newest.

- **Please update Factwright:** an update that's ready shows across the top of the window, with What's new, Remind me tomorrow and Restart now. Remind me tomorrow puts it off for the day, and it still installs the next time you quit.
- **One restart:** when a newer version comes out while an update waits, the newer one takes its place, so one restart brings you up to date. This starts with 0.18.1: an update already waiting in 0.18.0 still takes two restarts once.
- **Download progress:** the sidebar shows an update while it downloads, and how far it's got.
- **How many copies are in use:** the download site counts the update checks and updates that copies already make, by Mac or Windows and version only, with nothing about you or your copy. The app sends nothing new, and the privacy policy says what's kept.
- **Up to date underneath:** React 19.3.1 and other library updates.
