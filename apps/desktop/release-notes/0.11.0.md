Searches never bring back a job you've decided on, one search can focus on something narrower, and your projects and life outside work have a home.

- **A job found again is the job you have:** one you passed on, applied to or set aside never comes back as New, however the next search writes its company, title or link. It shows what the newest search found, and Merge with… joins the rare pair Factwright misses.
- **Focus one search:** in Find jobs, narrow just this search by kind of company, pay, where you'd work, industries and how recent, with notes of your own.
- **Beyond work:** in Profile, your projects in any field, and you outside work: interests, what you're learning, your communities. You choose how projects show on your resumes, and a project you keep private stays off everything you send.
- **A Projects page:** for a job your projects help with, ask for one page of them in your resume's look, as a list, cards or short case studies, beside the cover letter.
