Add job reads a job's link as you add it, and says plainly when a site can't be read.

- **Add job reads the link:** paste a job's link and Factwright reads the posting, fills in the company and role you left empty, and keeps the posting with the job, so Analyze job uses it as it is.
- **When a site can't be read,** Add job says why in plain words, and what works: open the posting in your browser and click Save to Factwright, or paste the description into the new Job description box. A site that couldn't be reached, or was busy, gets Try again. LinkedIn and Indeed stay off-limits.
- **More postings read right:** Taleo's posting rather than the site's menus around it, and big employers' postings on Ashby. A closed posting on Greenhouse, Lever and others says it has closed.
- **Only the posting is kept:** a link to a file, such as a PDF, or to a page of several jobs says so, rather than being kept as the job's posting.
