See what applicant tracking systems read in your resume, and fix it in a click.

- **ATS X-ray:** on any resume, see the text an applicant tracking system pulls out, in the order it reads it, the details it fills in, and anything that would trip it up, such as letter-spaced headings or a missing skills section. It's worked out on your computer, with nothing sent anywhere.
- **Keywords, honestly:** the X-ray checks the job's key terms against your resume. It says "add it" only where your fact bank backs the term, and "check it first" where it isn't sure.
- **Send for revision:** tick what the X-ray found and send it to your Claude. It fixes what your facts back, asks you about the rest first, keeps the old version, and the X-ray shows what got fixed. A resume you've already sent is never changed.
