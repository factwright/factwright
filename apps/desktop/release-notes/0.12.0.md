Templates you make from a resume you like now look like it, and you can report a problem from the sidebar.

- **Your template, your sections:** a template your Claude makes from a resume you like has that resume's sections and no others, and resumes made in it have only those too, with no band of numbers or highlight cards it didn't have. A template you made before keeps its look: for one that matches, drop the same resume on Profile → Resume templates again.
- **Report a problem:** at the foot of the sidebar, under Support Factwright. It opens an email in your own mail app, naming your version, for something wrong in the app or in what Claude wrote.
