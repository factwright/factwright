A job's panel says what to do next at every stage, and has a place for each kind of thing, with a button to make the first one.

- **Up next:** what to do next for the job, with the button that does it, from Run a fit check and Tailor a resume to Mark as applied, Prep for the call and Draft thank-you notes.
- **A place for everything:** fit across the top, with the pay, then your resumes and cover letters, the application's questions, people to contact and your questions for Claude, beside your progress, calls and notes. Empty ones show a + button to make the first.
- **Versions:** ask for changes to a resume or cover letter in your own words, or for a new version, and the one before is kept, to look at again, use again or remove. One you applied with stays as you sent it.
- **Reminders with a day:** add one in Progress, and it waits there with your calls until its day comes.
- **Claude's work folds away:** while Claude works, a bar says what it's doing, then what it made, with Open it.
- **Sorting, and the list beside an open job:** click a column to sort your jobs, and a job you expand keeps a slim list of the others beside it.
