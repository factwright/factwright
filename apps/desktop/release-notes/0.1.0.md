The first beta of Factwright, for Mac and Windows.

- **Your job hunt in one place:** the jobs you're considering, where each one stands, and dated notes from every call.
- **A fact bank you can read and fix:** every claim on your resumes comes from it, and you confirm each one.
- **Tailored resumes and answers,** written by your own Claude from your confirmed facts.
- **Sync** between your computers and your phone, locked so only your devices can read it.
