Answer your Claude's questions in Factwright, not the terminal.

- **Questions in a dialog:** when your Claude needs to ask you something, such as whether you've used a skill a posting wants, the questions open in Factwright, all at once: each option with what it means, a box for your own words, and a note for Claude. Your answers go straight to your Claude Code.
- **On your time:** put the questions aside with Not now and come back to them from Claude's panel, or answer them in the terminal, as before.
