Factwright's side of the browser extension's next version: applications that run over several pages, and filling without Factwright on the computer.

- **A page at a time:** with the extension's next version, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, iCIMS, Oracle Taleo and SmartRecruiters' newer form fill as you go from page to page, with a block for each job and school in your work history.
- **Filling through sync:** on a computer without Factwright, the extension's next version fills from your Profile, resumes and what you sent before, and can keep a new answer for next time. Each job's folder now syncs with the job, so it finds what it needs quickly.
- **What stays yours:** questions in a form's self-identification part, a box that declares what you sent is true, and a name a form asks as a signature are left for you.
