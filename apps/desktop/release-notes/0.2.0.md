Job statuses that say what you decided, profiles for a shared computer, and linking without a code.

- **Statuses that say what you decided:** New is a job you haven't touched. Claude's work on it moves it to To review, and only you move it on, to To apply, Later or Passed. The jobs page has seven views to match.
- **Sort a search quickly:** a new job leads with four quick choices, and you can select several jobs to move or pass them at once.
- **Profiles:** people who share a computer each keep their own job hunt, sync account and Claude login. Your job hunt so far is the first profile, untouched.
- **More than one kind of job:** look for, say, developer and project manager jobs in one profile, each kind with its own titles, levels, pay and starting resumes.
- **Claude's levels:** the sidebar shows the model and effort Claude runs at, and a Pro plan starts on lighter levels.
- **Link without a code:** approve a new computer or browser from a computer that's set up, once both show the same emoji.
