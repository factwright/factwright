Save to Factwright, the browser extension, is now in both the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons, and Factwright points to it however you sync.

- **Save jobs from your browser:** Save to Factwright saves a job straight to Factwright on this computer, with no account and no sync, or through sync from another computer. Factwright's links to it now show whichever sync you use, a server of your own included.
- **A guide to your own server:** How to set one up opens factwright.com/own-server, which walks you through running a sync server of your own for free in your own Cloudflare account.
