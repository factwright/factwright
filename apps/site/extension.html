Save to Factwright

Save the job you’re looking at to your Factwright job hunt, with its posting as you saw it, through end-to-end encrypted sync. For Chrome and Firefox.

Free and open source · Needs Factwright for Mac or Windows, with Factwright’s sync or your own server

How it works

Save a job the moment you find it.

It reads the job data sites publish, the layouts of LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday and other job sites, or the text you selected, so it saves postings you can only see signed in. On a site it doesn’t know, it reads the page’s main text, for you to check first. The job shows in Factwright on your computer as New, ready for a fit check, a tailored resume or a cover letter, and its posting stays even after the listing comes down.

  • The toolbar button

    On a job’s page, click it, check the title, company and posting, and save it. Pin it so it’s always there.

  • Alt+Shift+S

    Saves the job without opening anything. On a Mac, it’s Option+Shift+S.

  • The right-click menu

    Right-click the page and choose Save job to Factwright, to save it the same way.

Anything to check first, such as a company it can’t tell, waits for you in its popup: Chrome opens it, and Firefox marks its button with “!”.

Private by design

It reads a page only when you ask it to.

What it saves is locked with your job hunt’s own key before it leaves your browser, as everything you sync is, so our servers store it only in a form they can’t read.

  • Nothing as you browse

    It reads the tab you’re on only when you click its button, its menu item or its shortcut. Its popup sends nothing until you save.

  • One site, no tracking

    It reaches no site but your sync server, Factwright’s or one you run yourself, and has no analytics or tracking.

  • Your keys, in your browser

    It keeps your job hunt’s keys in your browser, where they can’t be copied out. They open your whole job hunt, so it’s only as trustworthy as its code, which is public and reviewed by each store. Link it only in a browser you use yourself.

  • LinkedIn, your call

    It reads a LinkedIn job you’re looking at only when you click to save it. Turn that off to save LinkedIn jobs by their link and title alone.

Our privacy policy says what it reads and keeps, and how it’s built is public.

Setting it up

Link it once, from Factwright on your computer.

It saves through sync, so it needs Factwright on your computer with sync on: Factwright’s sync, for $5 a month or $50 a year, or a server of your own, for free. Once it’s linked, it saves from that browser even with Factwright closed.

  1. Get Factwright for your Mac or Windows PC, and turn on sync from its sidebar.
  2. Add Save to Factwright to Chrome or Firefox, once it’s in their stores. Its setup opens on its own.
  3. Choose the sync Factwright uses, and sign in, then approve linking it in Factwright on your computer, once both show the same emoji.

Or link it with a link code from Factwright’s Sync settings, or with your recovery key.

For Chrome 127 or later, and Firefox 140 or later.
