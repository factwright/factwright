What it does

End-to-end encrypted: our server keeps your job hunt only in a form it can’t read.

Turning it on

Start from Factwright on your computer.

You subscribe in your browser, on Stripe’s checkout, never in the app.

  1. Get Factwright for your Mac or Windows PC. It’s free.
  2. Click Turn on… under Sync, then Sign in with your browser, under Factwright’s sync.
  3. Sign in with GitHub or Google, then subscribe, by the month or the year, all in your browser. Factwright carries on by itself.

Then add your other computers the same way, and link your phone from Factwright’s Sync settings. Already subscribed, or have sync for free? Factwright skips the paying. Running your own server? Choose Connect your server… instead, as the guide says.

Questions

Good to know.

What happens if I cancel?

Sync keeps going to the end of the time you paid for, then it pauses. Each computer keeps working with its own copy, and your synced job hunt stays on our server, still locked, until you delete your account. Your phone can still open it, and you can download all of it. Subscribe again, and sync picks up where it left off.

Can I get a refund?

Payments aren’t refunded, except where the law requires it, or if you were charged by mistake. Everything on one computer is free, so you can try Factwright first, and subscribe once you want sync.

Can I switch to yearly, or change my card?

Yes. From your account page in the web view, you can switch between monthly and yearly, change your card, cancel, and get your receipts.

What if I delete my account?

Your subscription ends straight away, with your account and everything you synced, and the time left on it isn’t refunded. To keep syncing to the end of what you paid for, cancel first, and delete your account after.

Who can see my job hunt?

Only you and your own Claude. Sync locks your job hunt on your computer before it leaves, with a key we never see, so our server keeps it only in a form it can’t read. Paying doesn’t change that: Stripe gets your email address and what you give it at checkout, never your job hunt. On your phone, it opens in your browser with code our server sends, as our privacy policy explains.

Is the rest of Factwright free?

Yes. The app, its updates and everything it does on your computer are free, and so is Save to Factwright, which saves through Factwright’s sync. Sync on our server is the one thing you can pay for. The work Claude does counts against your own Claude plan or API key.
