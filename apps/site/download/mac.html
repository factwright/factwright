Downloading Factwright for Mac

Your download starts in a moment, into your Downloads folder.

Didn’t start? Download for Mac

By downloading Factwright, you agree to its terms of service. Our privacy policy says what we collect.

Then, to install it

  1. Open the disk image from your Downloads.
  2. Drag Factwright to Applications, and open it from there.
  3. Choose Open if your Mac checks that you meant to open an app from the internet.

Factwright keeps itself up to date. It runs your own Claude Code: if that isn’t on your Mac yet, the app shows you how to get it. With sync on, our browser extension, coming soon, saves jobs from Chrome or Firefox too.

For Apple silicon and Intel Macs, on macOS 12 or later. Need it for Windows?
