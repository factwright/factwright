# Preferences (soft nudges)

The user's tastes, learned from their feedback on resumes, answers and messages. They're **defaults, not rules**.
- Apply them unless a job clearly needs something else. If you skip one, say why in that application's `notes.md`.
- **Precedence:** `rules.md` (binding) beats these nudges, and these nudges beat `writing.md`.
- **Seen 2+ times** is a strong default. **Seen once** is a lean.
- If the user contradicts a nudge later, the newest feedback wins: edit the entry and keep a short "was:" note.
- If the user says "always" or "never", ask whether it should become a rule in `rules.md` (and `lint.json` when it's checkable).

## Adding or bumping a nudge
The user may react in the same session or a later one.
1. Fix what they reacted to first.
2. Then add a nudge, or bump one that's there: raise Seen and update the date.
3. Confirm in one line: "Saved as a nudge: …".

Record their words, not your reading of them. Praise counts too: what they liked goes under **Keep doing**, so it's done again.

One line each:
`- **<nudge>**: Seen N · last YYYY-MM-DD · scope: all|startup|big-company|public-sector|leader|builder|ic|pdf|docx|letter · from: "<their words>" (<application folder>)`
