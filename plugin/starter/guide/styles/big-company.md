# Style: big-company

## Reader
An applicant system reads it first, then a recruiter matching it to the posting, then the hiring manager. They reward:
- results at scale, measured
- reliability, quality and risk control
- working across teams and functions
- the posting's own terms, matched

## Tracks
| Track | Start from | Highlights to lead with | Bullet groups |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leader` | `base/big-company-leader.json` | team size and growth, budgets or scope as `facts/` allows, measured outcomes | Leading teams · Results · Process and quality |
| `builder` | `base/big-company-builder.json` | the scale of what they built or ran, measured improvements | What they built · Reliability and quality · Leading through others |
| `ic` | `base/big-company-ic.json` | measured results of their own work | the job's own themes |

The look comes from `templates.md`, for established companies.

## Emphasize
- **Scale, measured:** how many people, customers, sites, dollars or hours, as far as `facts/` allows.
- **Quality and risk:** fewer errors, audits passed, compliance, safety, uptime.
- **Working across the company:** with other teams, leaders, customers and partners.
- **People leadership:** teams built, hired, trained and grown.
- **Decisions and their trade-offs.**
- **Projects, briefly:** a line each, and only those the posting's skills or domain call for.

## Avoid
- Startup slang: "scrappy", "hustle", "move fast and break things".
- Anything that sounds like a lone wolf. Frame work done alone as ownership, alongside working with others.

## Applicant systems
- The `leader` track carries a strengths list (`competencies`, about nine) that mirrors the posting's phrases.
- Mirror the posting's exact skill names, but only those in `facts/skills.md`.
- Big companies' sites often read Word files better: build both when the site or posting hints at it.

## Tone
Measured, specific, light on adjectives. Numbers over story.

## Sections
Header → Summary → highlights → strengths (leaders) → highlight cards → Experience → Education → Skills.
